Lizzy's Colorado Mountain Dream

Welcome to my Colorado Mountain Dreams! Exploring the beautiful scenic views of this state are magical.

My favorite spot I saw was Maroon Peak! I love the saying The mountains are calling.

It's not the mountains we conquer, but ourselves- Sir Edmund Hillary

My favorite activities in the mountains are:

  • Hiking is a great way to get exercise and stay healthy.
  • Hiking allows me to connect with nature and appreciate the beauty of the outdoors.
  • Hiking is a great way to spend time with friends and family.

Drawbacks with hiking are:

  1. A lot of bugs.
  2. Difficult terrain.
  3. Weather changes quickly.
